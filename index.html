What an operating system is

On its own, a computer is just metal and plastic. The operating system is the software that wakes it up: the desktop, the windows, the files and every app you open.

Windows and macOS are the famous ones — they cost money and keep their code secret. Our three finalists do the same job, for free.

Why free and open

Free means no licence to pay — on one computer or on all a hundred. Open means anyone can see how it works, run it, copy it and share it.

€0to put the same system on every PC

No software bill as the lab grows, and updates a teacher can carry from one PC to the next on a USB stick.

Who we’re choosing for

100identical desktops, installed once and used for years
1stcomputer ever for many users, with no Windows habits to fall back on
Lowpower and internet: electricity drops, connections are slow or missing

So the best system isn’t the most powerful one. It’s the one people can’t get lost in, and that keeps working when the internet doesn’t.

About 100 in, 3 out

We started from a long list of about a hundred free systems, mostly Linux distributions, and asked four questions.

  1. Is it free and open?Out: ChromeOS Flex. Free to use, but closed and built around Google services.
  2. Is it still updated?Out: dozens of small or abandoned projects. Without security updates a PC is a risk.
  3. Can a beginner use it?Out: Arch, Gentoo, FreeBSD. Great for experts, not for someone who has never held a mouse.
  4. Is it the best of its kind?Out: Ubuntu, Lubuntu, Puppy Linux. Good systems, but a finalist does the same job better.

Our pick

Endless OS

  • Works offline. Encyclopedia, lessons and apps are already on the PC.
  • Hard to break. Updates are all-or-nothing, and the previous version is kept.
  • Made for beginners. A phone-like home screen for people new to computers.

Plan B Linux Mint, wherever the internet is reliable and office skills matter.

1Download Endless OS

Pick the Full edition: its apps and content work offline. It needs a USB stick of 32 GB or more.

2Make a reformatter USB

In the Endless OS Installer for Windows, choose “Create Reformatter USB”. It can take a few hours.

3Boot the PC from the stick

On an Asus PC, hold F2 and press power to open the BIOS. Press F8 for the Boot Menu and pick the USB stick.

4Let it reformat

The stick copies Endless OS onto the PC. Unplug it and go to the next one: the same stick works again and again.

510 sticks, 100 PCs

With ten sticks, ten PCs install at once. Ten rounds and all 100 are ready.

After the install

  1. Train local championsTeach 5 to 10 people first; they help everyone else.
  2. Update by USBOne connected PC downloads updates; a USB stick carries them to the rest.

Fun fact

IBM z/OS

The heaviest system to run

z/OS is IBM’s operating system for its Z mainframes, the machines built for big business transactions. On top of the machine, IBM charges for the system every month.
