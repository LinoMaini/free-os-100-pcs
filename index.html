What an operating system is

On its own, a computer is just metal and plastic. The operating system is the software that wakes it up.

Why free and open

Free means no licence fee to pay. Open means anyone can see how it works.

Who we’re choosing for

100identical PCs for years of use
1stcomputer for many users; no Windows habits
Lowpower and internet: unreliable or missing

Our priority: easy to use, even without internet.

100 in, 3 out

About 100 systems, mostly Linux. Four questions:

  1. Is it free and open?Out: ChromeOS Flex — free, but closed.
  2. Is it still updated?Out: abandoned projects with no security updates.
  3. Can a beginner use it?Out: Arch, Gentoo, FreeBSD — for experts.
  4. Best of its kind?Out: Ubuntu, Lubuntu, Puppy — stronger finalists.

Our pick

Endless OS

  • Works offline. Encyclopedia, lessons and apps included.
  • Hard to break. All-or-nothing updates; previous version kept.
  • Made for beginners. A familiar phone-like home screen.

Plan B Linux Mint for reliable internet and office skills.

1Prepare the USB

Download Endless OS Full for offline apps.

2Install on one PC

Start the PC from the USB stick.
Install Endless OS and wait until it is ready.

3Repeat on 100 PCs

Reuse the USB sticks on the next computers.
10 sticks × 10 rounds = 100 PCs.
